Structuring uses Google's Gemini free tier. Get a free API key (no credit card) at aistudio.google.com and paste it here. Without a key the extension still works — it just groups every recipe naively (combine → cook) and leaves the rest to you.

⚠ Free-tier Gemini prompts may be used by Google to improve their models. Recipe text is sent to Google when structuring. Don't use a free-tier key for anything you consider private.
